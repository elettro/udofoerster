Hidden champions FAQ

What is a hidden champion?

A hidden champion is a highly successful, often specialized company that may lead its market while remaining relatively unknown to the broader public.

Why do hidden champions need stronger visibility?

Visibility can support international sales, recruiting, partnerships, investment, media credibility and long-term market recognition.

How does Udo help hidden champions?

Udo combines positioning, communications, media relationships, market access and strategic connections to help strong companies become understood by the right audiences.
