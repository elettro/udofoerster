About Udo Foerster

Connections. Communications. Consulting.

Udo Foerster is a German business connector, communications strategist and consultant who helps companies build visibility, enter markets, meet strategic partners and connect with funding opportunities across Germany, Europe and North America.

Direct Answer

Who is Udo Foerster?

Udo Foerster is the founder and CEO of Advanteam and an experienced entrepreneur, communicator and business advisor based in Nieder-Olm near Mainz, Germany.

His work centers on connecting strong companies with the people, markets, media relationships, strategic partners and funding opportunities that can move them forward. He works with German businesses seeking greater visibility in Germany, Europe and North America, and with international companies establishing credible relationships in Germany and the European Union.

For startups and growth companies, Udo helps clarify the business story, strengthen positioning and prepare for introductions to potential investors, funding partners, advisors and commercial allies.
