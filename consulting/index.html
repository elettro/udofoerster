Questions

Business consulting FAQ

What kind of consulting does Udo provide?

Udo advises on market entry, international expansion, business development, partner strategy, positioning, investor readiness and the relationships needed to support growth.

Does Udo replace a local sales team or legal advisor?

No. Udo helps leadership teams define strategy, identify gaps, coordinate the right specialists and establish the relationships needed to move forward.

Can consulting include both Germany and North America?

Yes. Udo works with German and international companies moving in both directions between Germany, Europe and North America.
