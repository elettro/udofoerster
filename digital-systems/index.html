The Strategic Role

Technology decisions need business leadership

Udo works between leadership, communications, data and development. He helps clients define the business purpose of a system before development begins.

The work covers what information the system should use, who should control it, how people will interact with it and how it should connect with the wider organization. Elettro provides user experience, architecture, development, integration and technical delivery.
