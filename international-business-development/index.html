Direct Answer

What does international business development involve?

It involves more than sales outreach. A company needs a relevant market position, trusted relationships, clear communications and a plan for converting conversations into sustained activity. Udo helps leadership teams connect these pieces so the market-entry and growth effort works as one coordinated program.
