How does Udo help with funding connections?

Udo helps a company become easier to understand and more credible before an introduction is made. Questions

Startup funding FAQ

Does Udo raise capital for startups?

Udo is not presented as a broker or guarantee of funding. He helps companies prepare their story, identify suitable relationship types and pursue relevant introductions to investors, funding partners, advisors and commercial allies.

What should a startup prepare before an investor introduction?

The company should be able to explain the problem, solution, market, proof, business model, team, capital need and expected use of funds clearly and credibly.

Can strategic partners be as important as investors?

Yes. A commercial partner, experienced advisor, customer or industry ally may improve credibility, traction and readiness before a funding conversation.
