Three Decades of Building What Lasts in the German Mittelstand

A practitioner, an entrepreneur, and a communicator — Udo Foerster brings thirty years of hands-on experience to the companies that form the backbone of Germany's economy.

When Udo Foerster founded advanteam in Aachen in 1999, he chose the name deliberately. It encodes a conviction he had held since his earliest years in business: real, lasting advantage is not an individual achievement. It is the product of teams that trust each other, communicate well, and work toward a shared goal.

That principle has guided three decades of entrepreneurship — from his studies at RWTH Aachen University and his early career in Germany's media industry, through positions with RTL, to the founding of his own communications and consulting firm. Today, advanteam is headquartered in Nieder-Olm near Mainz, advising companies on public relations, digital transformation, market positioning, and resilience.

Foerster is not a consultant who has never run a business. As a shareholder in the mein SANIHAUS group, he brings operational leadership experience to every engagement — grounding his strategic advice in the reality of entrepreneurship rather than abstraction.

"Communication, trust, and teamwork are not soft skills," he says. "They are strategic advantages." After thirty years, that message has only grown more relevant.

Entrepreneurship built on the right foundations.

Udo Foerster founded advanteam in 1999 with a conviction that the German Mittelstand — the backbone of Europe's largest economy — deserves world-class communications and strategic advisory support. That conviction has driven three decades of client work, entrepreneurship, and international media engagement.

Today, advanteam advises companies on public relations, digital transformation, crisis management, and business development. But the work always comes back to the same core belief: a company is only as strong as the team behind it.
