Three Decades of Building What Lasts in the German Mittelstand

A practitioner, an entrepreneur, and a communicator — Udo Foerster brings thirty years of hands-on experience to the companies that form the backbone of Germany’s economy.

When Udo Foerster founded advanteam in Aachen in 1999, he chose the name deliberately. It encodes a conviction held since his earliest years in business: real, lasting advantage is not an individual achievement — it is the product of teams that trust each other, communicate well, and work toward a shared goal.

That principle has guided three decades of entrepreneurship — from RWTH Aachen University to the German media industry, including RTL, to the founding of his own communications and consulting firm now headquartered in Nieder-Olm near Mainz.

Today, advanteam advises companies on public relations, digital transformation, market positioning, and resilience. As a shareholder in the mein SANIHAUS group, Foerster brings operational leadership experience to every engagement.

“Communication, trust, and teamwork are not soft skills,” he says. “They are strategic advantages.” After thirty years, that message has only grown more relevant.

Udo Foerster, Founder & CEO, advanteam, Nieder-Olm bei Mainz.

“A company is only as strong as the team behind it.”

Entrepreneur & Advisor

Udo Foerster founded advanteam in Aachen in 1999 after studying at RWTH and working with RTL and the Kölnische Rundschau. Today he leads from Nieder-Olm, advising businesses across Germany — while remaining active as a shareholder of the mein SANIHAUS group.

Strategy & Communications

advanteam combines public relations, business consulting, digital transformation advisory, and international market access — built around the conviction that sustainable growth is always a team achievement.
