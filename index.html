The coronavirus crisis, the war in Ukraine, and today's geopolitical situation present entrepreneurs with unprecedented challenges. The first step is always the same: review and secure your resources. From there, the path forward requires clear thinking, trusted counsel, and the right team.

Added to this is artificial intelligence. It offers enormous opportunities and potential — but also real risks when goals and expectations are set too high. Access to international markets is also becoming increasingly important, especially for small and medium-sized enterprises. advanteam helps you build the right contacts — with the right investors, the right clients, and the right partners.

Who He Is

Entrepreneur & Advisor

Udo Foerster founded advanteam in Aachen in 1999 after studying at RWTH and working with RTL. Today he leads from Nieder-Olm, advising businesses across Germany — while remaining active as a shareholder of the mein SANIHAUS group.

What He Does

Strategy & Communications

advanteam combines public relations, business consulting, digital transformation advisory, and international market access — built around the conviction that sustainable growth is always a team achievement.

Why It Matters

Germany's Hidden Champions Need a Voice

Germany's best companies are often its least-known. They deserve communications and advisory partners who understand their world — and who can help them lead it, nationally and internationally.
