Who He Is

Entrepreneur & Advisor

Udo Foerster founded advanteam in Aachen in 1999 after studying Economics at RWTH Aachen and working as a journalist at the Kölnische Rundschau and as Press Executive at RTL Deutschland. Today he leads from Nieder-Olm, advising businesses across Germany. He is also Commercial Director of the mein SANiHAUS group and publisher of Gesundheit-360.de.

What He Does

Strategy & Communications

advanteam combines public relations, business consulting, digital transformation advisory, and international market access — built around the conviction that sustainable growth is always a team achievement. As a FORBES Expert and internationally recognised voice, Foerster opens doors others cannot.

Why It Matters

Germany’s Hidden Champions Need a Voice

Germany’s best companies are often its least-known. They deserve communications and advisory partners who understand their world — and who can help them lead it, nationally and internationally.
